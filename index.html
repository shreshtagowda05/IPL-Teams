THE CHAMPIONS

The Iconic Moment

Royal Challengers Bengaluru ended their 18-year wait for winning the IPL trophy and they did it on the back of an emphatic win over Punjab Kings by six runs in the final. Rajat Patidar also became the first RCB captain to win the IPL

A Decade of Dreams Come True

For Virat Kohli and RCB, this win signifies more than just a trophy. It represents years of dedication, unwavering passion, and relentless belief. kohli,an icon for over a decade,has carried the hopes of millions through seasons of heartbreak and near misses.

This win is as much for the fans as it is for the team – Virat Kohli on RCB’s maiden IPL title

Virat said, “This win is as much for the fans as it is for the team. It's been 18 long years. I've given this time my youth, my prime. I gave it everything I have. I never thought this day would come. Was overcome with emotion as soon as the last ball was bowled.”
